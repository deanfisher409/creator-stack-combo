Contact

Talk to a human.

Creator Stack Combo is run by its operator — there is no support department and no ticket system. Email is the way to reach the person behind the app.

Email

hello@creatorstackcombo.xyz

For questions about the app, the TikTok connection, or the Terms and Privacy Policy. Please include what you were doing when the question came up, and which version of the stack you are running if you know it.

What to expect

Emails are read by the operator, not by an automated system. If your message is about your TikTok account, include only what is needed to answer the question — never send your TikTok password, and never send API keys by email.

Revoking access

You do not need to email to revoke access. In TikTok's settings, under authorized apps, remove Creator Stack Combo at any time. That immediately stops the app from reaching your account.
