FAQ

Questions, answered.

The questions creators ask most about Creator Stack Combo — what it is, what it needs, and what it does with a TikTok account.

Is Creator Stack Combo a website I sign up for?

No. It is software that runs on the creator's own computer, in Docker. There is no Creator Stack Combo account to create and no hosted dashboard. This website is only the public product page for the app.

Do I need a TikTok account to use it?

Yes — your own TikTok account, connected through TikTok Login Kit. The app only works with an account its owner has explicitly connected and authorized.

Does the app post to TikTok by itself?

No. Content is prepared as drafts in an approval queue. The creator reviews the exact draft — hook, caption, clip, and all — and decides what happens next. Nothing is published from a draft on its own.

Does the app see my TikTok password?

No. Sign-in happens only on TikTok's own pages. The app receives an access token from TikTok after the creator approves the permissions, and that token is encrypted and stored locally on the creator's computer.

What does the app access on my TikTok account?

Only what the approved permissions allow: basic profile information, profile stats, and the creator's video list, plus publishing videos the creator reviewed and approved. The full list is in the Privacy Policy.

Where does my data live?

On the creator's own computer. Drafts, clips, tokens, and logs are stored locally. There is no Creator Stack Combo server holding creator data.

Can I revoke the app's access?

Yes, any time. In TikTok's settings, under authorized apps, remove Creator Stack Combo. You can also delete the local token store on your computer. Both stop the app from reaching the account.

What computers does it run on?

macOS, Windows, and Linux, through Docker Desktop. The creator needs enough disk space for their video and clips; the exact requirements are in the stack's setup guide.

Does it work with other platforms?

This app is built for TikTok, using TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. It does not post to other platforms.

Does it buy followers or fake engagement?

No. It does not buy followers, reviews, or engagement, and it does not run engagement pods, mass direct messages, or automated liking and following.

Who do I contact with a question?

Email hello@creatorstackcombo.xyz. That address is monitored by the operator of this app.

Is Creator Stack Combo free?

There is no Creator Stack Combo subscription and no hosted service to pay for — the software runs on your own computer. Your own costs are the computer, disk space, and internet connection you already use to run it; no price is promised here, and nothing in this app asks you to pay to use it.

What happens if TikTok rejects the app?

If TikTok does not approve the app, the TikTok connection cannot be used until the issue TikTok raised is fixed and the app is approved. The local parts of the stack — drafting and clipping from your own video — do not depend on TikTok approval. There is no guarantee of approval, and review can take several days to two weeks.

Can I use it for Instagram or Facebook?

This build is for TikTok only, using TikTok’s official Login Kit and Content Posting API. Separate Instagram and Facebook editions of the stack exist — see the Changelog — but they are separate editions, not features of this TikTok build, and this build does not post to other platforms.

Is my TikTok account safe using it?

The app uses only TikTok’s official APIs, never asks for your TikTok password, and stores its access token encrypted on your own computer. You can revoke access at any time in TikTok’s settings. That said, no tool can guarantee how TikTok will treat an account: follow TikTok’s Terms of Service and Community Guidelines, keep your drafts honest, and do not use the app for anything TikTok prohibits.

Who is it for?

TikTok creators who publish regularly and want planning, drafting, and clipping in one local workspace — especially creators who repurpose long-form video into vertical clips, and creators who want to review the exact draft before anything goes out. It is built for one creator working on their own account, not for managing other people’s accounts.

How do I uninstall it?

Stop the stack, delete the Creator Stack Combo folder and its local data from your computer, and remove the app’s access in TikTok’s settings under authorized apps. Revoking access in TikTok stops the app from reaching your account even before you delete the local files. There is no hosted account to cancel, because there is no hosted account.
