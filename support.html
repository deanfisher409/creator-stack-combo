Still stuck?

Email the operator

Email

hello@creatorstackcombo.xyz

Include what you were doing when the problem came up, and which version of the stack you are running if you know it.

Never send by email

Never send your TikTok password, and never send API keys by email. If your message is about your TikTok account, include only what is needed to answer the question.

Revoke access yourself

You do not need to email to revoke access. In TikTok’s settings, under authorized apps, remove Creator Stack Combo at any time. That immediately stops the app from reaching your account.
