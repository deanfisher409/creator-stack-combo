Your TikTok workflow, on your machine.

Creator Stack Combo is a local-first TikTok creator operations tool. It runs on the creator's own computer, connects the creator's own TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, drafts content for human approval, and makes vertical clips with ClipForge — so planning, clipping, and review happen in one place the creator controls.

What it does

Four jobs, all local

Creator Stack Combo brings the everyday work around a TikTok account together on one computer, instead of scattering it across hosted dashboards.

Local-first

Runs on the creator's own computer

The agent, clip maker, and dashboards run locally in Docker. The creator's working files, drafts, and clips live on their machine — not on a Creator Stack Combo server, because there isn't one.

Login Kit

Connects the creator's own account

The app uses TikTok Login Kit to connect the account of the person running it. It only operates an account that person has explicitly connected and authorized, through TikTok's official sign-in flow.

Drafts first

Drafts content for human approval

Captions, ideas, and posting plans are prepared as drafts in an approval queue. The creator reviews the exact draft — wording, clip, and all — before deciding what happens next.

ClipForge

Makes vertical clips with ClipForge

ClipForge takes long-form video and renders vertical 9:16 clips for TikTok, with captions burned in. Finished clips come back as drafts for the same review step, not as finished posts.

Built around the creator's say-so

Creator Stack Combo is a tool for one creator working on their own account. Its limits are part of the design, and they're stated here so there's no ambiguity about what the app is for.

It does

  • Connect one creator's own TikTok account with Login Kit, with the permissions that creator approves on TikTok
  • Research trends and prepare content drafts, calendars, and clip ideas locally
  • Cut vertical clips from the creator's own long-form video with ClipForge
  • Keep drafts in an approval queue for the creator to review and edit

It does not

  • Create TikTok accounts, profiles, or personas for anyone
  • Operate an account that hasn't been connected and authorized by its owner
  • Buy followers, fake engagement, or run engagement pods
  • Collect TikTok passwords — sign-in happens only on TikTok's own pages
