Features

Everything in the stack.

Creator Stack Combo is five parts that work together on the creator's own computer: a creator operations agent, ClipForge, a dashboard, a 3D room front door, and a live-scene studio. Nothing here is a hosted service.

1 · The agent

Creator operations agent

The working brain of the stack. It runs locally, on a schedule, and prepares work for the creator to review.

Trend research

Scans trend sources on a schedule and scores what is gaining traction, so drafts start from what people are actually watching — not from guesses.

Draft generation

Turns strong trends into content drafts: a hook, a script outline, a caption, and hashtags, saved to the approval queue. Drafts are never published on their own.

Approval queue

Every draft waits in one place. The creator reads the exact wording, edits it, approves it, or deletes it. Nothing leaves the queue without the creator.

Content calendar

Planned posts laid out by day, so the creator can see the week ahead and move things around before anything is due.

Performance log

Views, likes, and follower changes for published videos, kept locally, so the next cycle's drafts learn from what actually worked.

Local memory

Past drafts, decisions, and results are stored on the creator's computer and feed the next round of planning. Nothing is sent to a Creator Stack Combo server.

2 · ClipForge

ClipForge — long video into vertical clips

ClipForge is the clipper inside the stack. It takes a long-form video the creator already owns and renders short vertical clips for TikTok.

9:16 vertical rendering

Clips are rendered in vertical 9:16 format, sized for TikTok's feed, with the subject kept in frame.

Captions burned in

Spoken words are transcribed locally and burned into the clip as captions, timed to the audio.

Scene-aware cuts

Clip boundaries follow real scene changes in the source video, so a clip starts and ends where the moment does.

Review before use

Finished clips come back as drafts in the same approval queue as the text drafts — the creator watches the clip before deciding anything.

3 · The dashboard

Observe panel — see what the agent is doing

One page that shows the agent's current status, recent activity, drafts, training, and the TikTok connection — with a chat box to talk to the agent and give it feedback it can learn from.

Status at a glance

Whether the agent, ClipForge, and the local model are up, and what the agent is working on right now.

TikTok connection card

Shows whether TikTok is connected, which account, which permissions, and when the token expires — and a Connect button when it isn't.

Chat and feedback

Ask the agent questions or leave criticism and ideas. Feedback is stored and used by the learning loop.

Diagnostics and recovery

Plain-language health checks with fixes, and one-click recovery for stuck jobs — no command line needed.

4 & 5 · Front door & live studio

Bits-Room and Live Scene Studio

Two more parts of the stack, for the creator's own computer.

Bits-Room

A 3D room front door for the stack, with a small local agent that works on one approved folder on the creator's computer. Anything it wants to change needs the creator's explicit approval first.

Live Scene Studio

A desktop control panel for live streaming setups, with an overlay sized for TikTok LIVE. It controls the creator's own broadcast software locally — it does not post or automate TikTok itself.
